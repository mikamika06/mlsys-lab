We need to implement a minimum viable data-free knowledge distillation (KD) pipeline to study how a smaller student model can be trained entirely on synthetic data generated by a teacher, without access to the original training corpus.

You will build the three phases of this process:

1. **Teacher self-sampling**: The teacher will be represented by a vocabulary of states and a logits matrix. You must convert these logits to transition probabilities (using softmax) and generate a synthetic sequence of states by autoregressively sampling from it.
2. **Low-rank student distillation**: We simulate distillation without slow SGD loops by fitting a student model directly on the visited states. Compute the optimal student predictions via a truncated SVD (low-rank) approximation exclusively on the rows of the teacher's logits that were visited. Unvisited rows remain zero. Compare the Mean Squared Error (MSE) when the student is fitted on this synthetic data versus a real corpus.
3. **Minimum diversity bound**: If we theoretically select states optimally (in descending order of their L2 norm), calculate the minimum number of unique states we must sample to guarantee a specific `target_mse`. The total MSE bound must account for both the unvisited states (where error is their full norm) and the SVD truncation error on the states we *do* visit.
