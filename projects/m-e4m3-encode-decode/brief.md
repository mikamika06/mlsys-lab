Our low-precision attention pipeline in the FlashAttention stack is currently experiencing severe numerical instability and excessive quantization noise during fp8 forward passes. When processing large activation tensors through the attention layers, intermediate query-key dot products exhibit sudden catastrophic overflow or severe underflow, leading to NaN outputs and complete loss of gradient convergence in training runs.

Profiling the kernel execution reveals that our current baseline byte-packing routines for the e4m3 format are failing to correctly quantize input ranges, mapping large dynamic range outliers improperly and truncating crucial precision bits. Furthermore, when attempting to reconstruct dequantized tensors using per-tensor scaling factors, the recovered activation matrices deviate significantly from their original FP16/BF16 counterparts, producing high mean squared error spikes across multi-head attention blocks.

Additionally, the dynamic scale search mechanism currently employed is sluggish and suboptimal, frequently selecting scaling factors that clip valid numerical distributions or fail to maximize the representational range of the 8-bit format. We need a robust, deterministic, and highly accurate implementation of the e4m3 codec, per-tensor descale reconstruction, and an optimal scale search routine to stabilize low-precision attention execution without sacrificing throughput or introducing numerical degradation.
