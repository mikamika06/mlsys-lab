We are running large language models in FP8 precision on NVIDIA Hopper GPUs to maximize training and inference throughput, but our attention layers are suffering from severe numerical instability and catastrophic attention score corruption. When processing inputs with high-variance activations or sudden high-magnitude outliers, the standard dynamic quantization scaling factors fail to capture the distribution accurately, leading to large relative errors in the attention softmax denominator and completely scrambled output tokens.

Furthermore, simply casting activations to lower precision without addressing prominent outlier channels causes catastrophic precision loss in the key-value cache and query-key dot products. We need a robust mechanism to evaluate and control FP8 attention error relative to a reference bf16 baseline, specifically integrating Hadamard incoherence processing (randomized Hadamard transformations) to spread out extreme outliers prior to quantization, and comparing the numerical behavior directly against unquantized bf16 execution on Hopper hardware architectures.

Your task is to implement the FP8 attention error analysis pipeline, incorporate Hadamard incoherence transformations to mitigate outlier-induced quantization noise, and construct a rigorous test suite that verifies error bounds and catches flawed Hadamard projection matrices or faulty scaling logic.
