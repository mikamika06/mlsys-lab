The performance profiling dashboard for our fused attention kernels has reported persistent anomalies when analyzing hardware utilization metrics across varying sequence lengths in our production environment. Specifically, engineers noticed that automated evaluation scripts processing recorded forward and backward throughput logs are failing to accurately identify the exact sequence length where our custom kernel performance crosses over and outperforms the baseline FlashAttention-2 implementation. Furthermore, numerical validation jobs evaluating online softmax scaling mechanics are occasionally reporting divergent accumulated attention denominators and incorrect output tensors during multi-head attention training passes. These discrepancies surface unpredictably during routine regression testing under high-load sequence scenarios, leading to unstable performance reporting and downstream numeric verification failures across multiple hardware targets. The system requires robust modules to correctly parse recorded throughput curves, determine precise crossover points, and correctly model the mathematical outcomes of online softmax scaling operations, accompanied by a dedicated regression test suite to catch structural calculation errors.
