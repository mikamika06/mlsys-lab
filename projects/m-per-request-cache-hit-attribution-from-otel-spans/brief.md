Operators notice that our production OpenTelemetry tracing logs show dense spans for inference requests, but our billing and internal caching dashboards show zero correlation between reported prefix cache hits and actual GPU memory reuse. When we try to trace individual requests to understand why some prompts trigger high cache reuse while identical-looking requests miss entirely, the span attributes are either missing or incorrectly aggregated across concurrent batched requests. Specifically, child spans representing block allocation and prefix matching inside the serving engine fail to propagate request-level correlation IDs or misattribute cache-hit tokens when requests are chunked or re-ordered in the continuous batching scheduler. We need to implement a robust per-request attribution pipeline that ingests raw OTel span payloads, correctly maps span events and attributes back to individual request contexts, computes accurate cache-hit and total-token metrics, and exposes verifiable metrics without corrupting under concurrent load or losing trace links.
