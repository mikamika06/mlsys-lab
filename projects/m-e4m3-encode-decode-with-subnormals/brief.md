An inference server serving a large language model with FP8 quantization (E4M3 format) is exhibiting incorrect token generation and numerical divergence when processing inputs containing extreme outliers, particularly in long-tailed activation tensors. When inspecting the internal representations of activations, developers noticed that values falling into the subnormal range or near the maximum representable bounds are either saturating prematurely or losing precision unexpectedly during the encode and decode cycle.

Additionally, downstream kernels are reporting scaling factor mismatches, leading to severe underflow or overflow when scaling back to higher-precision formats. The current ad-hoc quantization logic fails to properly account for the specific bit-layout constraints of E4M3, including its asymmetric positive/negative dynamic range, the handling of subnormal values where the implicit leading bit is zero, and the derivation of per-tensor scaling factors based on maximum absolute values.

Your task is to implement a robust, correct E4M3 quantization and dequantization pipeline that handles subnormals correctly, derives accurate per-tensor FP8 scaling factors, and compares the behavior of E4M3 against E5M2 and INT8 formats under long-tailed activation distributions. You must write the implementation modules, verify each milestone through the test harness, and provide regression tests that ensure safety against incorrect subnormal scaling implementations.
