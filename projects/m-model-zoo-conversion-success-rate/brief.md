Our edge deployment infrastructure is currently suffering from recurring failures during the automated model conversion phase within our nightly CI/CD pipelines. Engineers have noted that when large batches of candidate models from our central model zoo are processed for export into edge-compatible formats, conversion jobs frequently terminate abruptly or hang indefinitely without providing clear diagnostic feedback. The logs produced during these failures are densely populated with unstructured error traces that obscure the underlying root causes, making it nearly impossible to determine whether a failure originated from an unsupported tensor operation, an invalid dynamic shape configuration, or a memory exhaustion event during flatbuffer serialization. Furthermore, our current diagnostic and inspection scripts rely heavily on massive, heavyweight deep learning framework runtimes solely to parse basic signature definitions from model files. This heavy dependency footprint severely degrades pipeline performance, causes frequent out-of-memory crashes on resource-constrained build agents, and introduces unacceptable latency into our daily workflow. Finally, we lack a unified mechanism to reliably compute and track model conversion success rates over time, leaving us blind to performance regressions and making it difficult to verify whether recent pipeline fixes actually improve overall export stability.
