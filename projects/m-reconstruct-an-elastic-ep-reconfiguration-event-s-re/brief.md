TITLE: Process OOMs and stale weights during elastic MoE scaling

We've been seeing critical failures when dynamically scaling our Mixture of Experts (MoE) training jobs. When the cluster autoscaler triggers a scale-out or scale-in event, the intermediate state manager emits a sequence of expert reconfiguration operations to migrate expert shards across the active node pool. However, shortly after a complex migration batch, several worker ranks consistently report Out of Memory (OOM) errors, while others show converging loss curves plateauing entirely.

Memory dumps from the crashed nodes reveal that they are holding onto expert shards they were supposed to have offloaded hours ago. Meanwhile, the routing logs indicate that some tokens are being dispatched to ranks that never actually loaded the required expert, resulting in zeroed-out gradient updates. The issue only manifests after a long chain of concurrent "move", "add", and "remove" operations are processed by the load balancer's event tracker. We need to verify how these event trajectories are being applied to the routing table. It looks like the resulting layout drifts from reality, either leaking replicas during migrations or failing to track their new homes correctly.
