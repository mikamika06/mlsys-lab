During our recent scaling runs of large Mixture-of-Experts models across multiple nodes, we noticed severe degradation in collective communication efficiency during the dispatch and combine phases of expert parallelism. Specifically, job telemetry reports that network utilization spikes unpredictably, and our estimated buffer requirements diverge significantly from actual peak memory allocations during long-context training passes. Furthermore, when auditing our recorded dispatch logs to debug load imbalance across expert ranks, we found that our downstream scripts fail to correctly reconstruct per-rank token distribution from the raw routing traces, leading to miscalculated communication volumes and mismatched tensor shapes during all-to-all exchanges. At the same time, comparative benchmarking between our tensor parallelism and expert parallelism collectives reveals erratic throughput discrepancies that cannot be explained by standard network bottlenecks alone. We need a robust implementation to mathematically derive exact communication volumes, accurately parse and reconstruct token counts from dispatch logs, and properly model or evaluate EP vs TP throughput characteristics to restore cluster stability.
