We recently attempted to migrate our production embedding layers to FP8 and INT8 block scaling, hoping to shave down memory bandwidth overhead and overall generation latency. However, early offline experiments have been an unmitigated disaster. The final model accuracy metrics fall off a cliff when evaluated on our new long-tail mixed-magnitude embedding distributions. In addition, we are seeing sporadic NaNs and huge numerical drift accumulating during the forward pass.

The kernel team suspects that the quantize-matmul-dequantize kernel isn't properly isolating the extreme outliers into separate spatial blocks, which causes fallback to per-tensor scaling artifacts where one exceptionally large embedding activation completely crushes the numerical precision of the entire layer.

We need you to implement an exact numpy baseline of our per-block INT8 matrix multiplication, derive the exact empirical expected relative error bounds for FP8 E4M3 representations on our value distributions, and finally build a reproducible regression proof demonstrating the catastrophic precision drop between per-tensor and per-block scaling on an outlier-heavy array. This will form the essential mathematical foundation and safety net for validating the actual low-level Triton kernels we will write next week.
