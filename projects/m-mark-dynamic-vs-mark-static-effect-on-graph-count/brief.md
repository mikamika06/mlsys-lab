Ticket: Excessive Graph Recompilations in Dynamic Shape Workloads

Symptoms: During serving and inference workloads using `torch.compile`, our pipeline experiences severe latency spikes and high memory overhead due to repeated graph recompilations. When input tensor dimensions such as batch size or sequence length vary across requests, `torch._dynamo` triggers a new compilation cycle for almost every unique shape combination. This causes the total graph count to balloon rapidly, exhausting cache limits and stalling execution. We have inconsistent application of `mark_dynamic` and `mark_static` across our model inputs and intermediate tensors. We need a robust mechanism to correctly apply dynamic and static shape markings per tensor dimension, evaluate the resulting compilation graph count under diverse shape trajectories, and ensure that our graph caching policy remains strictly bounded and deterministic.
