We are working on low-level tensor override tooling in `mlsys-lab`, specifically dealing with gguf and llama.cpp-style model weights and overrides. When users want to configure tensor mappings or target specific layers for offloading or adjustments via command-line flags, they often use patterns or specific flags like `--cpu-moe`.

In our newer tensor override subsystem (`ot`), the old command-line arguments are being replaced by a unified, regular expression-based parameter syntax. Currently, engineers cannot easily map the legacy `--cpu-moe` flag to its equivalent `-ot` regular expression pattern. This leads to configuration mismatches when attempting to route Mixture of Experts (MoE) tensor layers to CPU memory while keeping attention or dense layers on GPU or vice versa.

Your task is to implement the regex parser and conversion utilities in the `ot` package so that legacy `--cpu-moe` command-line switches and options are correctly translated into the corresponding regular expression equivalents. You will define the tokenization and translation rules, implement validation checks, and ensure that a comprehensive regression test suite catches any malformed or overly broad regex patterns that might accidentally match non-MoE weights or leave crucial expert layers unhandled.
