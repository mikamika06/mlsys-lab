When building automated CI regression checks for our PT2E quantization pipeline on custom models, engineers noticed that analyzing graph node distributions before and after `prepare_pt2e` and `convert_pt2e` is yielding inconsistent node counts. Specifically, our graph metrics script fails to reliably categorize and count FX graph nodes (such as `call_module`, `call_function`, `call_method`, and inserted observer/quantization nodes) when exported graphs contain folded modules or composite operations. Furthermore, differential reports comparing original, prepared, and converted graph structures report mismatched node deltas across different torchao quantization flows, causing our downstream verification harness to throw unexpected lookup errors and fail silently during nightly evaluation runs. We need a robust, deterministic analyzer module that correctly parses torch.fx graphs or mock graph representations, accurately counts node types, tracks transformations across prepare and convert phases, and maintains strict structural invariants.
