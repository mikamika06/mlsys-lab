During mixed-precision fine-tuning runs with large learning rates on deep transformer models, training logs suddenly flatline or report loss as NaN around step 450. Concurrently, memory-efficient 4-bit quantize-dequantize weight caches exhibit subtle drift over extended evaluation horizons, leading to unexplained degradation in downstream task accuracy compared to unquantized baselines. The engineering team has observed that sudden gradient spikes trigger numerical overflow in the master weights before standard backpropagation completes, while iterative quantization loops accumulate systematic rounding residuals. We need robust verification utilities to guarantee that gradient clipping bounds are strictly enforced and to quantify the exact numeric error introduced by repeated NF4 quantize-dequantize cycles. This task requires implementing core low-level numerical stability checks, precise gradient norm scaling algorithms, and deterministic simulation loops to isolate and prevent these silent failure modes during distributed fine-tuning sessions.
