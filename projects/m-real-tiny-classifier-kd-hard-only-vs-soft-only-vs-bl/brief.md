Production deployments of our tiny student classifier are struggling to match the teacher model's accuracy on edge devices, despite our initial implementation of Hinton knowledge distillation. Benchmarking reports indicate that our training pipeline yields erratic convergence behavior when switching between hard labels, soft logits, and blended loss formulations. Specifically, the validation accuracy delta between blended distillation and pure soft-label training is unexpectedly compressed, suggesting an underlying formulation or scaling bug in how temperature and loss weights are applied in the custom training loop. Engineers note that standard cross-entropy baselines sometimes outperform our distilled student, which invalidates the entire pruning and compression pipeline for our upcoming release. We need a robust implementation of hard-only, soft-only, and blended distillation losses, coupled with a precise training routine and a comprehensive regression test suite to enforce correct loss scaling and temperature scaling invariants under all operating conditions.
