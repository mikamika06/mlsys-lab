Our model training and inference pipelines are suffering from severe latency spikes during the initial warm-up phase and whenever batch shapes fluctuate. Profiling logs indicate that the graph executor is repeatedly re-invoking the compiler frontend, invalidating cached execution artifacts far more often than expected. Engineers attempted to mitigate this by tweaking the compilation mode flags, but switching between strict static specialization, automatic dynamic shaping, and fully dynamic compilation has yielded contradictory cache behavior. Furthermore, our offline capacity planning tools fail to accurately predict when guard cache evictions will trigger costly recompilations under a finite LRU policy. We need a robust analysis module to quantify recompile frequencies across different dynamic settings and a faithful simulator to model LRU guard-cache dynamics under varying workloads.
