Production telemetry from our MoE serving cluster shows unexpected latency spikes and non-linear throughput scaling when servicing multi-turn long-context requests across distributed nodes. The infrastructure team recently deployed expert parallelism with expert parallel load balancing (EP+EPLB) in vLLM, but capacity planning reports indicate that token generation throughput diverges significantly from theoretical estimates as the EP degree increases. Furthermore, side-by-side benchmarking against alternative serving engines such as SGLang exhibits unexplained variance in total execution time and per-token routing latency during peak request loads. Operators need a unified performance toolsuite to parse recorded vLLM execution logs, calculate load imbalance metrics over time, extrapolate throughput scaling curves across higher EP degrees from empirical benchmark records, and compare fine-grained execution trace statistics between vLLM and SGLang runtimes to isolate bottlenecks in our deployment pipeline.
