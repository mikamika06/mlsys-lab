When evaluating structured pruning pipelines under aggressive compression targets, systems engineers frequently observe a severe degradation in model performance across incremental pruning ratios. Recently, telemetry from our evaluation harness revealed that while single-point pruning assessments appear stable, automated sweeps measuring accuracy degradation versus pruning ratio fail to reliably capture post-hoc fine-tune recovery dynamics. Specifically, post-recovery evaluation runs report anomalous flatlines or sudden performance collapses that do not align with empirical validation checks on reference workloads. Operators running automated compression benchmarks note that without rigorous trajectory tracking of both pre-recovery and post-recovery accuracy bounds across varying sparsity levels, regression suites cannot distinguish between genuine recovery failures and instrumentation skew in evaluation loops. This ticket requires implementing robust measurement utilities and curve evaluation logic to accurately capture, compute, and verify accuracy versus pruning ratio curves alongside fine-tune recovery trajectories, ensuring deterministic and precise profiling across all compression milestones and guarding against silent metric corruption.
