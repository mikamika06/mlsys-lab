We are implementing context parallelism and ring attention primitives for FlashAttention, specifically focusing on the zigzag token assignment generator, communication volume model, and overlap feasibility check under the `m-zigzag-assignment-generator` unit.

During distributed sequence-parallel attention runs across multiple devices, standard contiguous block-to-rank assignments lead to severe load imbalances in causal attention masks. Because earlier tokens attend to very few keys while later tokens attend to the entire sequence, contiguous chunking leaves devices handling the initial tokens idle or underloaded during later stages of computation, whereas devices holding the tail of the sequence bear disproportionate communication and compute burdens.

To resolve this bottleneck, we require a zigzag token assignment generator that interleaves forward and backward token blocks across ranks in a mirrored serpentine pattern. However, adopting zigzag assignments changes the communication topology and introduces strict data-dependency constraints for ring-based p2p token exchanges. Specifically, the system must compute accurate communication volume models for overlapping key-value transfers with local matrix multiplications, and execute precise overlap feasibility checks to guarantee that asynchronous ring communication steps can be fully hidden behind GEMM execution phases without stalling the pipeline.

Your task is to implement the core modules in `zigzag/assignment.py`, `zigzag/comm.py`, and `zigzag/overlap.py`, ensuring correct index generation, precise byte-volume estimations, and robust feasibility validation under various sequence lengths, ring sizes, and causal mask configurations. You must also author comprehensive regression tests in `tests/test_regression.py` that capture these vital system invariants.
