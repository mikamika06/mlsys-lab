We are experiencing critical scheduling and resource tracking inefficiencies in our low-level serving infrastructure when handling concurrent requests with mixed sequence lengths, chunked prefills, and decode iterations. During high-throughput serving workloads, our batch argument builder fails to properly pack sequence offsets, resulting in misaligned tensor strides and incorrect pointer arithmetic passed into the flashattention kernels.

Furthermore, our current scheduling and memory tracking layer lacks an accurate analytical model for Time to First Token (TTFT) and Inter-Token Latency (ITL) under chunked-prefill workloads. When prefills are split across multiple iterations to meet latency SLAs, the calculated memory footprint, effective batch sizing, and queue scheduling heuristics diverge from actual hardware performance. This leads to unpredictable queuing delays, suboptimal compute utilization, and severe tail latency spikes under heavy concurrent load.

Your task is to implement a robust mixed-batch call-argument builder, handle precise chunked-prefill offset math, and build a reliable TTFT and ITL analytic model to ensure optimal scheduling and execution efficiency.
