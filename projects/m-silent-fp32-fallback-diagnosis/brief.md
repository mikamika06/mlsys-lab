We recently integrated PyTorch 2 Edge Export and PT2E quantization routines into our production mobile deployment pipeline to shrink our LLM footprint and accelerate inference on edge accelerators. However, after exporting and converting several large model variants, our telemetry and performance profiling have revealed severe performance anomalies. Specifically, inference latency on device is significantly higher than expected—almost matching our unquantized full-precision baseline—despite logs indicating that quantization was successfully applied. Furthermore, memory bandwidth utilization remains bottlenecked during weight loading layers, suggesting that weights may not be utilizing the intended low-bit representation. Upon inspecting exported model graphs, we noticed that execution time is heavily concentrated in specific linear operators where intermediate precision checks or node attributes differ subtly from expected quantized patterns. Our automated testing harness reports passing quantization passes, yet model inspection shows certain weights or operators executing in full precision without any explicit warning or diagnostic error raised by the compiler runtime. Additionally, when attempting to configure fine-grained weight quantization using custom group sizes and torchao-style layouts, our memory budgeting calculations diverge wildly from on-device footprint measurements, leading to unexpected out-of-memory errors during runtime initialization. We need a robust diagnostic suite to uncover these silent fallbacks, automatically determine optimal int4 group sizes under strict error budgets, and precisely calculate effective bits-per-weight for complex quantization configurations.
