When deploying optimized deep learning models through TensorRT and ONNX Runtime toolchains, post-training quantization calibration passes are consistently failing accuracy parity validations against native floating-point baselines. Production logs indicate that maximum absolute value calibration fails to handle extreme outlier distributions across high-dimensional activation tensors, resulting in quantization scale factors that either prematurely clip critical feature activations or severely underutilize the available integer dynamic range. Furthermore, entropy-based calibration utilizing Kullback-Leibler divergence minimization suffers from incorrect threshold searching routines and non-monotonic distribution binning, leading to improper probability distribution alignment on activation tails. Additionally, NVFP4 block-scaled quantization round-trip implementations exhibit unexpected precision loss, scale factor overflow, and alignment errors due to incorrect sub-block scaling factor computation and block-wise layout mapping. These compounding calibration and quantization errors prevent production models from satisfying strict error thresholds, resulting in severe accuracy degradations and deployment failures across complex workloads.
