Our serving tier is preparing to transition its first wave of heavy production models from standard eager PyTorch execution to compiled AOTInductor packages to minimize first-token latency and reduce serving overhead. During staging verification, however, the engineering team noticed erratic behavior regarding cold-start times and export compatibility across different architectural variants.

When deploying the models using `torch.export` followed by AOTInductor package generation, certain dynamic shape configurations and layers trigger unexpected compilation stalls, graph breaks, or incorrect shape constraints that only manifest when executing the packaged artifact for the first time. Specifically, models with intricate attention masking or conditional tensor sizing fail to export cleanly, or they produce packages where the cold-start initialization incurs a massive penalty due to unoptimized kernel caching and shape inference overhead.

We need a rigorous diagnostic and packaging pipeline that analyzes these issues systematically. You must implement a module that isolates and verifies clean model export compatibility, measures and compares eager versus AOTInductor package cold-start performance invariants, and derives the exact shape assertions and constraints implied by `torch.export` dynamic shapes. The package must accurately report whether candidate models are export-clean, extract explicit shape bounds from dynamic dimensions, and validate that packaging invariants hold under perturbed runtime inputs without relying on wall-clock measurements.
