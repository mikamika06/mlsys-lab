# Ticket: Production Latency Regressions and Excessive Build Times Stemming from Unmanaged TensorRT Timing Caches and Uncalibrated Builder Optimization Levels

## Symptom
Our deployment pipeline has recently begun exhibiting severe instability and extended deployment latencies during model compilation phases. Specifically, when deploying new TensorRT engines across our serving clusters, initial startup times have spiked unpredictably, with certain model variants taking upwards of twenty minutes to compile while others finish in seconds. More critically, even after generating a timing cache, subsequent cold-start builds frequently fail to achieve the expected latency reductions, suggesting that the serialized timing cache is either being ignored, invalidated, or failing to capture the optimal kernel configurations.

Compounding this issue, automated benchmark runs in staging have reported sporadic performance regressions where identical model weights and configurations yield vastly different inference latencies across consecutive runs. Operators note that these discrepancies appear tied to builder optimization levels and non-deterministic kernel tuning choices (tactics) that slip through validation without flagging. Because we lack a principled mechanism to quantify the build-time payoff of the timing cache, identify the optimal builder optimization knee where diminishing returns set in, and systematically filter or detect flaky tactics, our release pipeline remains blocked. We need a robust diagnostic and configuration module to model these trade-offs, enforce optimization thresholds, and safeguard against unstable tactics.
