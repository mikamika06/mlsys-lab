We are receiving complaints from the deployment team that our performance dashboard for edge inference is reporting impossible numbers. When exporting our hybrid vision-language models to mobile devices, the dashboard claims 95% of the model is being accelerated on CoreML or XNNPACK. However, the actual measured end-to-end latency suggests the acceleration is closer to 50%.

The profiler outputs a flat model graph where partitioned subgraphs are collapsed into opaque `DELEGATE` operations. It seems our telemetry is blindly counting the number of nodes in the graph instead of the computational weight (FLOPs) hidden inside those delegates. The delegate blobs are encoded as raw bytes containing the backend name, the number of ops, and the total FLOPs.

We need you to write an extractor that parses these blobs, and a measurement tool that correctly calculates the fraction of total FLOPs that have been delegated. Furthermore, looking at the partitioned graphs, we suspect our greedy partitioners for XNNPACK and CoreML are leaving performance on the table or grouping incorrectly. Please implement the partitioners so that they correctly group contiguous supported operations into a single delegate, allowing us to generate reliable benchmarks moving forward.
