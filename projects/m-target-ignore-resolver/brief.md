During large-scale mixed-precision quantization runs for Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, operators encounter catastrophic numerical degradation and unexpected divergence during validation. Inspection of intermediate layer activations reveals that sensitive routing components and gating mechanisms—which require high-precision numerical stability to correctly distribute tokens across expert networks—are being accidentally caught by broad wildcard targeting patterns and compressed into low-bit integer formats alongside standard attention and feed-forward projections.

When routers or gating layers are quantized, token routing decisions become unstable, leading to severe token dropouts, NaN propagation, and degraded perplexity scores. The compilation and quantization pipeline currently lacks a robust target and ignore resolution module capable of parsing complex inclusion and exclusion patterns, constructing precise architectural ignore lists specifically tailored for MoE structures, and automatically auditing quantized module maps to flag improperly compressed routing components before model serialization. This unit requires you to implement a target/ignore resolver, a specialized MoE ignore list constructor, and a detection mechanism for wrongly-quantized routers, protected by a comprehensive regression test suite.
