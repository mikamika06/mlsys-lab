When exporting PyTorch models to ONNX and TensorRT FP16/INT8 execution providers, automated deployment pipelines repeatedly pass converted models that later produce corrupted outputs during live inference. Standard validation based solely on absolute or relative tolerances fails to capture directional drift, while single metric thresholds permit localized floating-point spikes that explode in downstream layers. Conversely, overly strict maximum error checks reject valid low-precision optimizations due to benign individual outliers.

We need a numerical validation engine that measures Signal-to-Quantization-Noise Ratio (SQNR) in dB and Cosine Similarity between reference and target execution outputs. The engine must evaluate these metrics alongside maximum relative error inside a composite accept/reject gate to ensure runtimes meet precision thresholds without false positives. Additionally, to identify where precision degrades across deep networks, the engine must inspect per-layer activation chains, tracking error amplification ratios from layer to layer to pinpoint runaway quantization noise before deployment.
