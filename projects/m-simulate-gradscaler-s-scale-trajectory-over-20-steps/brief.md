# Ticket: GradScaler Scale Trajectory and Tracking Desynchronization

During recent staging runs of our mixed-precision training pipeline under high-throughput workloads, we observed that the internal logging and state tracking of the GradScaler gets completely desynchronized. Specifically, the metrics pipeline expects to correctly log the scale trajectory over a fixed window of 20 steps to ensure that scaling bounds are functioning properly.

However, when intermittent overflow events occur (where gradients contain infs or nans and cause a skipped update), our current telemetry fails to accurately model the backoff factor application and the critical reset of the contiguous successful step count. Furthermore, the downstream diagnostic tool that identifies which specific iteration indices were skipped due to overflows is currently returning misaligned step identifiers.

Finally, our predictive scheduler uses the historical overflow sequence to derive the absolute step index where the next scale-doubling should theoretically occur, but it is frequently off by several steps, leading to suboptimal checkpoints and unnecessary loss spikes. We need a purely deterministic simulation module that perfectly mirrors the GradScaler's internal step tracking. It must correctly simulate the exact scale values used across a given 20-step boolean sequence of overflows, precisely identify the skipped step indices, and accurately predict the next scale-doubling index based on the remaining successes needed. This will fix the tracking bugs across our distributed training clusters and restore confidence in our scaling metrics.
