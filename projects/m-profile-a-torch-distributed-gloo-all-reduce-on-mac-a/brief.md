Ticket #8421: Profiling anomalies in distributed training traces on macOS development nodes.

During local development and validation runs of distributed training workloads using the gloo backend on macOS, engineers have observed inconsistent performance metrics when inspecting execution traces. Specifically, automated performance dashboards report erratic self-time measurements for collective communication primitives such as all_reduce, making it difficult to isolate whether performance bottlenecks originate from compute kernels or synchronization overheads. Furthermore, as teams experiment with different parallelization strategies, automated continuous integration pipelines fail to correctly distinguish between communication patterns characteristic of data-parallel architectures versus those typical of tensor-parallel layers within trace logs. This ambiguity leads to misclassified optimization recommendations, incorrect hardware utilization alerts, and confusion during local profiling sessions where multi-process communication is emulated over local loopback interfaces. The profiling and analysis utilities must be stabilized to reliably extract self-time durations and accurately classify trace topologies without requiring physical multi-node clusters.
