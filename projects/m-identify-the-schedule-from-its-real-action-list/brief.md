# Ticket: Pipeline Parallelism Monitoring Mismatch in Distributed Training Runs

We are observing severe discrepancies between our expected training throughput and the actual measured step times across multi-node Megatron-LM runs using advanced pipeline parallelism configurations, including standard 1F1B, interleaved 1F1B, and recent Zero-Bubble variants. Specifically, our telemetry pipeline receives raw execution action traces and log dumps from worker ranks, but our automated health-check dashboard fails to reliably categorize which schedule is actually executing under load.

When inspecting clusters running zero-bubble or complex interleaved schedules, the dashboard reports erratic bubble fractions—sometimes calculating negative idle times or misattributing forward-backward interleaving blocks as standard GPipe bubbles. This inaccuracy prevents our automated profiler from flagging synchronization stalls and memory footprint imbalances before they cause hard job failures or silent performance degradation in production clusters.

We need robust parsing and classification logic embedded directly into our low-level system tooling (`pipesched`). The utility must accurately inspect raw action lists to identify the exact scheduling strategy being executed, compute precise empirical bubble fractions from verbose Megatron execution logs without floating-point drift, and maintain strict invariants that can be verified via automated regression tests. Resolving this will ensure our training observability tooling correctly attributes execution latency and detects scheduling anomalies across heterogeneous GPU topologies.
