Your task is to analyze how PyTorch handles static versus dynamic shapes, trace the origin of automatic-dynamic markings from a shape trace, and fix the data-dependent symbol error by incorporating `torch._check` assertions correctly. This ensures that the dynamic shape engine properly records the necessary runtime constraints without triggering unsafe guard generation or falling back to costly graph recompilations. You will implement the corrected model logic, provide utility functions for predicting dynamic shape marking behavior, and author a robust regression test suite that catches regressions where shape guards are improperly omitted.
