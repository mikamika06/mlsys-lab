# Ticket: Production service suffers from severe startup latency spikes due to unoptimized PyTorch compilation caching

## Symptom
Our distributed inference fleet is currently experiencing intermittent multi-minute latency spikes whenever container instances are restarted or autoscaled. Profiling indicates that worker nodes are repeatedly invoking JIT compilation via TorchInductor from scratch, completely bypassing expected local disk caches. Furthermore, even when local caching directories are preserved across warm restarts, minor updates to model weights or embedded scaling constants trigger full compilation cascades instead of incremental cache hits. Finally, in our multi-container staging environment where pre-compiled binary artifacts are bundled and distributed to isolated worker pods, the runtime environment still triggers redundant recompilation passes upon initial execution. This results in degraded throughput, inflated pod initialization times, and frequent health-check timeouts during high-traffic deployment windows. We need a robust measurement and caching verification harness to diagnose compile latency characteristics, validate cache hit/miss behavior under constant modifications, and ensure artifact persistence mechanisms yield zero recompiles in fresh execution environments.
