# Ticket: Excessive Dynamic Shape Recompilations in Production Serving

## Symptom
Our model serving infrastructure is experiencing severe throughput degradation and intermittent latency spikes during high-concurrency inference workloads. Telemetry dashboards show that PyTorch compilation overhead has spiked dramatically, with multiple worker processes spending up to forty percent of their CPU time inside the graph compiler and guard evaluation routines rather than executing kernel operations. Tracing logs indicate that incoming request tensors with slightly varying sequence lengths and batch dimensions are constantly triggering fresh graph recompilations instead of reusing existing compiled artifacts. 

Specifically, minor variations in dynamic input shapes are causing the runtime to generate an unmanageable number of specialized compiled graphs, completely exhausting our predefined recompile budget. Furthermore, incorrect or overly restrictive guard conditions on tensor attributes are invalidating cache entries prematurely, forcing redundant compilation cycles even for requests that fall within acceptable operational bounds. We need a robust bucket scheduling and shape specialization mechanism that constrains unique graph recompilations within our strict budget while correctly handling shape, value, and identity guards without sacrificing numerical correctness or execution efficiency.
