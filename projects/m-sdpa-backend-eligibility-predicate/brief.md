# Ticket: PyTorch SDPA Dispatch Mismatches in Production

We are observing severe performance regression and incorrect kernel fallback behaviors in our inference pipeline when deploying large language models with varying sequence lengths and data types. Specifically, when executing scaled dot-product attention (SDPA) across different hardware capability levels and attention configurations, PyTorch's internal dispatch mechanism occasionally selects sub-optimal or incompatible attention backends.

For instance, certain sequence lengths and head dimension configurations that should be eligible for FlashAttention or memory-efficient attention are unexpectedly falling back to the slower math kernel, or vice versa, leading to unexpected memory bloat and latency spikes. Furthermore, when analyzing performance regression tickets submitted by our engineering team, our automated telemetry and profiling tools fail to reliably extract and verify which specific SDPA backend kernel actually executed from raw execution traces, making root-cause analysis extremely difficult.

We need a robust, deterministic suite of modular utilities within our model runtime stack to explicitly compute backend eligibility predicates, accurately predict the dispatch decision ahead of execution given specific tensor and hardware parameters, and reliably parse profiler traces to identify the executed backend. This will ensure our dispatch logic behaves predictably, adheres strictly to hardware constraints, and allows automated testing of attention execution paths before production deployment.
