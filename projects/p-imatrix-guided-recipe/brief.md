Ticket: Low-bit quantization degrades model accuracy and introduces severe perplexity spikes in sensitive layers. Standard uniform quantization treats all weights equally, ignoring their actual contribution to the activation flow and output distribution. We need to implement an importance matrix (imatrix) guided quantization pipeline that collects activation statistics over a representative text corpus, computes per-tensor weight sensitivities, and guides the quantizer to allocate higher precision to critical blocks. You will build a complete reference implementation that constructs the imatrix, evaluates its impact on quantization block selection, compares perplexity (PPL) and Kullback-Leibler Divergence (KLD) metrics against uniform baselines, measures the net quality gain, and demonstrates sensitivity to corpus selection. Finally, you will write robust regression tests to ensure correctness under edge cases and prevent silent regressions in future iterations of the quantization recipe.
