When scaling up token generation workloads under high concurrency in our LLM serving engine, we observed significant performance degradation and unexpected latency spikes during the token decoding phase. Specifically, iterative single-token decoding steps in the eager execution path suffer from excessive kernel launch overhead and low hardware utilization, failing to hit our target tokens-per-second SLA. While attempting to mitigate this by integrating CUDA graphs to amortize launch latency, engineers noticed inconsistent speedups, and under certain dynamic batching configurations, the measured throughput ratio between CUDA graph execution and the eager baseline drops unpredictably or errors out entirely. Operators report that GPU utilization oscillates wildly between low-batch decode iterations, and profiling traces show suboptimal scheduling. We need a robust measurement and execution harness that cleanly isolates eager decode performance from CUDA graph decode performance, accurately computes the throughput ratio across diverse input scenarios, and ensures strict performance invariants are maintained under all operating conditions without regressions.
