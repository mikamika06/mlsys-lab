We are observing severe memory bloat during prolonged training and evaluation runs across several PyTorch workloads. Over the course of hundreds of iterations, host or device memory consumption inches upward steadily without stabilizing, eventually triggering Out-Of-Memory (OOM) kills or severe system throttling. Standard monitoring tools show a persistent, per-step upward trend in allocated bytes or active tensors, yet simple reference counting has failed to pinpoint where tensors are accumulating or which iteration step introduces the retention.

Engineers report that standard profiling utilities produce overwhelming amounts of raw telemetry without clear classification of memory states across distinct execution phases or checkpoints. We need a systematic mechanism to analyze sequence metrics, classify a series of memory snapshots captured at regular checkpoints to identify leaks versus expected caching behavior, and instrument training loops to cleanly dump and evaluate memory footprints without altering execution semantics. The solution must provide robust programmatic detection of upward trends, precise multi-snapshot classification, and a reliable regression testing suite to protect against future memory regression issues.
