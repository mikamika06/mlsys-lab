We are experiencing significant training instability during intermediate-layer feature distillation in our transformer models. When using standard Mean Squared Error (MSE) loss between student and teacher hidden states, the loss frequently suffers from sudden spikes and scale imbalances, particularly across deeper layers where residual stream variance grows rapidly. This often leads to gradient explosions and convergence plateaus during mixed-precision training runs.

We need to investigate alternative loss objectives, specifically comparing hidden-state MSE against cosine similarity loss, and analyze how different layer-mapping choices impact the overall feature-loss magnitude and optimization trajectory. Your task is to build a robust utility module that computes these distillation losses, implements flexible layer-mapping strategies, evaluates training stability metrics, and includes a comprehensive regression test suite that ensures loss scaling and mapping correctness under various failure modes.
