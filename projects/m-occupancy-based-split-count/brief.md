During high-throughput online decoding with FlashDecode, profiling reveals unexpected latency spikes and accuracy anomalies when serving requests with small batch sizes and long context lengths. Under low batch concurrency, the GPU Streaming Multiprocessors (SMs) are severely underutilized when running standard single-pass attention kernels. Switching to a split-KV approach improves GPU occupancy, but operational telemetry shows two degrading symptoms. First, under certain workload configurations, total decode latency increases rather than decreases due to sub-optimal split selection and excessive intermediate state memory overhead. Second, when partial outputs are combined across sequence splits, numerical deviations emerge in output log-sum-exp values, causing downstream regression failures in model sampling and temperature scaling.

We need to build a structured module in `splitkv/` that dynamically determines occupancy-based split counts, models reduction and compute costs to select ideal split parameters, and executes numerically exact online softmax reductions across intermediate split outputs.
