We are implementing a custom PyTorch CUDA graph training step capture and replay mechanism for a lightweight iterative training loop, but we are running into subtle correctness and safety issues. When attempting to wrap a training step loop inside `torch.cuda.is_current_stream_capturing()` or using manual graph capture utilities, the execution either throws hard capture-safety errors or produces silently incorrect weights and gradient values.

Specifically, during graph capture, certain operations such as dynamic tensor allocations, non-graph-safe host-device synchronizations, or improper handling of mutated input/output buffers cause severe failures. In particular, we observe that tensor outputs are overwritten incorrectly by subsequent runs or fail to update model weights due to static address binding in the captured graph workspace. Furthermore, our test suite currently lacks a proper regression safeguard to detect if future changes reintroduce state corruption during graph replays. We need to implement a robust hand-captured training step module with correct workspace isolation, tensor management, and a rigorous regression test suite to ensure capture safety and correct gradient updates.
