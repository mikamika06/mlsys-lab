When profiling a standard training or inference loop, engineers notice that the training throughput drops significantly compared to expectations, and the PyTorch profiler key averages table reveals unexpected performance degradation. Specifically, the table shows a dominant CPU time consumption that puzzles the development team. Despite the model architecture being completely standard and utilizing efficient layers, a low-level operation repeatedly surfaces at the top of the self CPU time metrics, dominating the execution trace. The team needs to parse key average tables, correctly extract and rank the top-5 hottest operations by their total self CPU time, and write a robust regression test that flags when specific synchronization bottlenecks are inadvertently introduced by careless tensor extractions inside repetitive loops. Without proper automated checks, these performance regressions slip silently into production models, degrading latency and scaling efficiency across distributed training nodes.
