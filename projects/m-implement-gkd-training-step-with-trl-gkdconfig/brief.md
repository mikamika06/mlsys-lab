Our distillation training pipeline using Generalized Knowledge Distillation (GKD) is exhibiting erratic loss spikes and unpredictable distribution collapse during student fine-tuning runs. When evaluating training steps across configured divergence modes (Forward-KL, Reverse-KL, and JSD), loss values do not scale smoothly with temperature adjustments, causing optimization instabilities at lower temperatures. Furthermore, mode switching in GKDConfig generates identical loss values across symmetric and asymmetric logit configurations, suggesting that divergence calculation fails to preserve mode behavior on toy probability distributions.

In addition, our evaluation harness for tracking on-policy versus off-policy exposure bias reports flat sequence drift metrics regardless of the sampling mixture parameter `beta`. Autoregressive rollout trajectories show zero cumulative shift over sequence steps even when the student model heavily deviates from teacher prefixes.

We need a clean, verified implementation of GKD step loss using GKDConfig parameters and a sequence distribution drift measurement module that correctly captures mode asymmetry and sequence-level trajectory shift.
