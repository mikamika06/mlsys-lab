An experimental sequence-level distillation pipeline is failing during the policy gradient update step when integrating with the Hugging Face TRL library's Generalized Knowledge Distillation configuration. Engineers working on on-policy distillation have noticed that training runs using the default sequence-level objective experience unexplained divergence and unexpected gradient behavior when switching between distribution divergence measures across different generations.

Specifically, when configuring the training step to use generalized distillation via `GKDConfig`, the loss computation does not correctly handle the interpolation parameter beta between forward-KL, reverse-KL, and Jensen-Shannon divergence modes. On synthetic toy distributions where the teacher and student are modeled as categorical probability vectors, the gradient update fails to stabilize when the policy drifts on-policy versus off-policy. The system currently lacks a clean reference implementation of a single GKD training step that accepts a `GKDConfig` object, computes the correct divergence based on the specified beta parameter, evaluates toy distribution behaviors, and measures the exact distribution drift between on-policy rollouts and off-policy data distributions.

Your task is to implement the GKD training step utilities, analyze toy distribution divergence modes across forward-KL, reverse-KL, and JSD, and build a robust regression safeguard test suite that catches incorrect divergence computations and faulty drift metrics.
