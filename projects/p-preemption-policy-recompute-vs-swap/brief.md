When our serving engine hits peak traffic and starts preempting requests, the tail latency (p99) skyrockets to unacceptable levels. We are currently resorting to either recomputing the KV cache from scratch for every preempted request, or blindly swapping everything to CPU RAM over PCIe. Neither of these pure policies works efficiently across our diverse traffic mix. For short sequences, recomputing is slowed down by prefill overhead, making swap much faster. For long sequences, the PCIe bandwidth bottleneck makes swapping out and back in prohibitively expensive compared to a recompute pass.

We need a dynamic preemption policy powered by an accurate cost model. Please implement latency estimators for both recompute and swap paths based on compute throughput, PCIe bandwidth, and constant transfer overheads. Then, calculate the context length breakeven point and implement a policy that selects the optimal action for any given sequence length. Finally, ensure your regression tests rigorously enforce that the new dynamic policy performs strictly better than both the pure recompute and pure swap baselines on mixed workloads.
