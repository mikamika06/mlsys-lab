An experimental distributed training cluster running long-context language models is experiencing severe memory bottlenecks and unexpected scaling plateaus when pushing sequence lengths beyond 32k tokens. While the underlying hardware topology features high-bandwidth interconnects across multiple compute nodes, the naive implementation of self-attention across distributed chunks causes massive memory inflation and high latency overheads from uncoordinated communication patterns.

Engineers investigating the cluster telemetry noticed that when sequence chunks are distributed across a ring of processors, the naive tensor exchanges block downstream computation unnecessarily and fail to exploit overlapping communication with backward/forward passes. Furthermore, when switching to an all-to-all alternative, the hidden dimension redistribution introduces communication volume crossover points depending on the sequence length, tensor parallel size, and hidden dimension size, leading to suboptimal performance regimes.

Your task is to build a robust low-level distributed attention engine from scratch that implements Ring Attention, precisely manages the real All-to-All Ulysses reshuffle across token and head dimensions, and computes the exact communication volume crossover boundaries between Ring Attention and DeepSpeed-Ulysses. You must provide clean modular primitives, a complete reference pipeline, and a comprehensive regression test suite that guarantees your parallel communication invariants hold under perturbed execution paths.
