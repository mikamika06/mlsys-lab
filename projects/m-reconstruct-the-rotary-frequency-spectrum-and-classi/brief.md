During long-context evaluations on an optimized transformer architecture utilizing Rotary Position Embeddings (RoPE), we noticed catastrophic attention degradation and complete loss of retrieval capability when sequence lengths exceeded a specific threshold. Profiling the embedding generation revealed unexpected numerical anomalies in the high-dimensional components and sharp discontinuities in the calculated inverse frequencies. Specifically, when position indices grow beyond standard training bounds, the computed rotary frequencies wrap around or produce severe precision loss, leading to identical representations for distinct tokens. Additionally, certain head dimensions fail to correctly separate low-frequency context channels from high-frequency fine-grained position channels, causing multi-head attention scores to collapse into uniform noise. Your task is to implement a robust utility to reconstruct the rotary frequency spectrum, accurately classify dimensions into appropriate frequency bands, and write a regression test suite that reliably catches position-id overflow failures and incorrect spectrum computations under stress conditions.
