We are running our FlashAttention deployment benchmarks on a specialized edge cluster to verify whether our theoretical throughput claims hold up under real hardware constraints. Recently, engineers have raised concerns that the published numbers in our quarterly report might be overly optimistic or computed using inconsistent arithmetic definitions of Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) and GPU theoretical peaks.

Furthermore, some reports calculate Model Flops Utilization (MFU) against peak half-precision tensor core capacity, while others use mixed-precision or non-fused baseline rates, leading to speedup claims that fail when audited end-to-end.

Your task is to implement a robust measurement and accounting module that correctly calculates MFU from raw measured latencies, reproduces the exact published TFLOPs/s performance numbers using proper arithmetic intensity bounds, and audits inconsistent speedup claims by isolating hardware utilization anomalies from arithmetic inflation. You must ensure your implementation correctly parses execution logs, applies proper hardware constraints, and passes all regression invariants.
