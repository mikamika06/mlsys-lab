Our disaggregated prefill-decode cluster is exhibiting severe tail-latency spikes and pipeline stalls during high-concurrency request bursts. Operators report that when switching our serving architecture from monolithic instances to a disaggregated setup—where prefill nodes stream KV caches over network fabrics to decode nodes—requests with short prompt lengths experience unexpected queuing delays, while long prompts seem to saturate the interconnect links completely. Looking at cluster telemetry, the end-to-end request latency calculation fails to account for the precise serialization overhead and transmission windows required to push KV states across 25 Gbps, 100 Gbps, and 400 Gbps links. Furthermore, capacity planning teams are currently misjudging the minimum prompt length threshold where disaggregation overhead actually pays off compared to local processing, leading to suboptimal cluster sizing ratios between prefill and decode instances. We need a rigorous analytical module to compute exact KV transfer sizes, transfer times across various network speeds, analytical break-even points, and optimal prefill-to-decode capacity ratios without guessing.
