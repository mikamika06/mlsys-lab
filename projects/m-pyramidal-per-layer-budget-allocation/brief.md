# Ticket: Address Accuracy Collapse in Long-Context KV Cache Compression via Pyramidal Allocation

## Symptom
During long-context autoregressive serving and needle-in-a-haystack retrieval evaluations, applying a uniform compression ratio or aggressive eviction policy across all transformer layers leads to severe and unexpected accuracy degradation. Specifically, models experience catastrophic forgetting and retrieval failures because intermediate layers are stripped of necessary token representations, whereas early syntactic grounding layers and late extraction layers require significantly higher fidelity and preservation budgets. Furthermore, serving infrastructure currently lacks an automated mechanism to perform equal-budget comparative press bake-offs across competing allocation strategies on standardized prompt sets, making it impossible to systematically establish optimal compression-to-accuracy Pareto frontiers or validate whether per-layer distribution profiles strictly adhere to structural invariants. Without an integrated testing harness and robust regression safeguards, custom allocation scripts frequently introduce silent budget violations, non-monotonic layer weightings, or invalid token distributions that compromise serving reliability in production clusters.
