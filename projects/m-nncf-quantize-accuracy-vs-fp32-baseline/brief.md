CPU inference throughput and memory footprint metrics for our deployed model serving pipelines have consistently exceeded our strict production budgets following export to OpenVINO IR formats. When attempting to resolve these resource constraints by applying post-training INT8 quantization via NNCF, our engineering team has encountered severe challenges in verifying model fidelity and performance gains reliably. Specifically, evaluation scripts fail to compute and validate output accuracy degradation relative to the FP32 baseline, while metrics tracking storage byte reductions and execution speedups via benchmark utilities show inconsistent scaling across different optimization runs. Without a standardized evaluation harness, we are unable to guarantee that quantized models maintain acceptable relative error boundaries or confirm expected performance dividends. We require a robust evaluation and benchmarking module to programmatically measure relative accuracy error, quantify IR storage footprint reduction ratios, and compute benchmark execution speedups.
