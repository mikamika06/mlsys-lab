During W4A16 quantization trials for our new custom LLM family deployed on edge inference accelerators, downstream perplexity degrades unexpectedly when converting linear projection layers using naive round-to-nearest or standard second-order error-updating heuristics. Furthermore, when attempting to protect salient activation channels by applying channel-wise scale factors, several key-value and feed-forward projection layers produce numerical overflow, NaN tokens, or severe activation distortion during FP16 matrix multiplications, leading to immediate inference runtime crashes.

To make matters worse, our current quantization engine fails whenever it encounters unseen custom module topologies with non-standard naming conventions. It falls back to isolated single-layer processing, completely missing the fact that parallel projection pairs (such as gate and up projections or multi-head attention inputs) share common activation streams and require unified scaling vectors.

We require a robust quantization suite that compares MSE distortion across RTN, GPTQ, and AWQ algorithms, automatically inspects non-standard architecture configs to derive correct input-sharing layer mappings, and computes activation-aware channel scale factors with explicit safeguards against scale explosion.
