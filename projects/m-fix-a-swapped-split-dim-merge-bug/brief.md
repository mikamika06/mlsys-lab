When working with distributed checkpoints, specifically converting DeepSpeed sharded states to a universal format, our checkpoint conversion pipeline has been exhibiting subtle data corruption issues during tensor reconstruction. Engineers report that certain multi-dimensional parameter tensors, particularly those involving tensor parallel column or row splits, end up with corrupted shapes or transposed slice layouts after conversion. Downstream training runs fail to load these checkpoints properly, throwing shape mismatch exceptions or producing catastrophic divergence during initialization because weights across attention and MLP projections appear scrambled or misaligned. We need to systematically verify how checkpoint fragments map back to parameters, predict the exact output layout of our conversion scripts under various sharding layouts, and implement a robust test suite that explicitly guards against swapped dimension merging bugs during the reconstruction process.
