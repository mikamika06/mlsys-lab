An open-source checkpoint loading and quantization pipeline is failing during the round-trip phase when working with compressed-tensors checkpoints. When trying to load a saved compressed state dict and serialize it back out, the resulting tensor values drift significantly beyond acceptable numerical tolerances, and the reconstructed scaling factors or compressed representations fail to align with the expected format specifications.

Our current evaluation harness shows that checkpoints serialized through the pipeline exhibit high discrepancy when compared against uncompressed reference weights or standard round-trip loaders. Specifically, the round-trip conversion fails to preserve the structural attributes and numerical precision required for downstream low-level ML systems, causing errors in the maximum absolute difference metrics across quantized layers.

Your task is to implement a robust round-trip conversion mechanism for compressed-tensors checkpoints. You must ensure that the compressed state dicts can be correctly unpacked, modified or verified, and repacked without violating structural invariants or introducing excessive numerical error. You will build the core processing modules, verify the correctness across milestones, and supply a comprehensive regression test to guard against silent regressions in checkpoint format handling.
