An LLM post-training pruning task has been deployed for one-shot unstructured pruning using weight magnitude, SparseGPT-style second-order updates, and Wanda (Weight and Activation) scoring. During initial evaluations on an evaluation server, engineers noticed that pruning quality metrics—specifically the perplexity degradation and relative error against uncompressed weight targets—diverge heavily depending on whether the calibration dataset matches the downstream domain. While magnitude pruning is domain-agnostic, it performs poorly on retention of hidden representations. Conversely, Wanda and SparseGPT achieve much lower relative errors on matching domains, but when exposed to out-of-domain calibration sets (e.g., calibrating on code or biomedical text while evaluating on general news or dialogue), their activation-weighted scoring vectors misalign, causing unexpected catastrophic error spikes in specific layers. We need you to build a diagnostic and benchmarking unit that compares magnitude pruning, SparseGPT-style updates, and Wanda pruning over recorded weight and activation matrices, and provides automated debugging checks for mismatched calibration domains.
