We are observing severe observability blind spots in our ONNX Runtime pipeline when deploying models with the TensorRT Execution Provider (TRT EP). During recent profiling runs on our inference clusters, production logs show unpredictable latency spikes and inconsistent execution paths, but our monitoring systems fail to extract or quantify how many subgraphs are successfully offloaded to TensorRT versus falling back to the default CUDA Execution Provider.

Furthermore, engineers tuning multi-session workloads report that timing-cache reuse across sequential session initializations is either failing or not being properly measured, leading to repeated costly engine build phases that degrade startup times. Without an automated way to parse diagnostic logs, perform head-to-head comparisons of CUDA EP versus TRT EP latencies, and track timing-cache hit metrics, we cannot diagnose whether performance regressions stem from fragmented subgraph partitioning, disabled engine caching, or improper EP prioritization. We need a robust analytics module to parse logs, compute accurate latency ratios, and verify cache reuse behaviour under strict regression tests.
