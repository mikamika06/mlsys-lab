Engineers report that after exporting a transformer model for edge deployment via coremltools, a few specific layers (such as output projection or certain attention blocks) stubbornly remain in FP16 precision instead of dropping to INT8 or INT4 as requested by the global quantization recipe. This unexpected retention blows past our tight hardware memory footprint and causes memory bandwidth bottlenecks on the neural engine. Worse, when we try to enforce a global size budget programmatically, the automated per-layer search either over-compresses critical layers leading to severe accuracy degradation, or fails to meet the strict model size budget entirely. Additionally, our recent W8A8 activation quantization calibration pipeline produces clipping artifacts and scale mismatches during runtime activation streaming, leading to sporadic NaN outputs on edge devices. We need a robust implementation that analyzes why layers retain FP16, performs an optimal per-layer bitwidth search under a strict size budget, and correctly calibrates W8A8 activation and weight scales without dropping precision invariants or introducing calibration drift.
